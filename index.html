Privacy Policy for SwingOut

Effective date: 25 September 2026 · Version 1.2

Developer: Leiso Studio · Contact: ferhatozkan7@gmail.com

1. Summary

SwingOut has no user accounts and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites; the game itself runs entirely on your device. Two things do leave your device. First, anonymous gameplay analytics: the App sends a small set of events — for example that level 12 was started, and that it was completed in 48 seconds with 14 moves — to Google Analytics for Firebase, so that we can see which levels are too hard and where players stop playing. Those events are tied to a randomly generated app-instance identifier, never to your name, e-mail address or an account. You can switch them off in Settings. Second, optional rewarded ads: when you run out of lives or want an extra hint, the App can offer to show you a short video ad from Google AdMob in exchange for the reward. An ad is only ever shown when you tap the button that asks for it, and you can always take the free path instead (restart the level, or play on without the hint). Section 6 explains what the ad service receives.

2. Who we are

SwingOut ("the App", "the Game") is developed and published by Leiso Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information, including the analytics described in section 3 and the advertising described in section 6. If you have any question about it, write to ferhatozkan7@gmail.com.

3. Information we collect

We ask you for nothing: the App has no registration, no login and no field in which you could type personal information. The App does not collect, request, or transmit:

  • names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses;
  • user accounts, logins, passwords, or social-network profiles;
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS) for tracking purposes — the App never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the identifier is not available to us or to our advertising partner for tracking you across other apps;
  • precise location — the App never uses Location Services and asks for no location permission;
  • contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any other device content;
  • payment or financial information — the App contains no purchases.

The App requests no runtime permissions and is not able to read personal content from your device.

What the analytics SDK does collect

To understand how the game is played, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase. While Analytics is switched on, the following leaves your device:

  • Installation identifiers. A randomly generated app-instance identifier created by the SDK, a Firebase installation identifier the SDK registers for this copy of the App, and, on iOS, the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV). They identify an installation, not a person; they disappear when you delete the App, and switching Analytics off in Settings discards them too.
  • The gameplay events we define. level_start, level_clear, level_fail, hint_used, theme_selected, and the advertising funnel events ad_offered, ad_started and ad_rewarded (which record only that an ad was offered, started or completed, and where in the game), with parameters such as the level number, elapsed seconds, number of moves, the minimum number of moves for that level, the star rating, the number of wrong moves, whether a hint was used, the rotation direction, whether the attempt was a retry, and the identifier of the selected visual theme.
  • Events the SDK measures automatically, such as first launch, app opens, app updates, sessions and session duration, and screen views.
  • Technical attributes of the installation: device model, operating system and version, app version, device language, and the store the App was installed from.
  • An approximate location — country, region and city — which Google derives from your device's masked IP address. The App never uses Apple's Location Services, and we never see your IP address.

No free text, no user identifier and no content you create is attached to these events. We do not set a user ID, we do not use Google signals, our Analytics property is not linked to any Google Ads account, and age, gender or interest categories are not available to us.

You can turn this off. Open Settings in the game and switch Analytics off. Collection stops immediately, any events still waiting to be sent are discarded, and nothing further leaves your device. You can switch it back on at any time. (Switching Analytics off does not affect the advertising service described in section 6, which only runs when you choose to watch an ad.)

4. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves game data in the standard local storage of your operating system (Unity PlayerPrefs, which maps to UserDefaults on iOS). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It consists only of:

  • level progress, star ratings, and best move/time results;
  • achievement progress;
  • the number of lives remaining in the level you are playing;
  • the state of a level you left half-finished, so you can resume it: the moves you made so far, the elapsed time and the blocked moves;
  • whether the App has already asked you to rate it;
  • preferences such as sound, music, haptics, and the selected visual theme;
  • whether you have switched Analytics off in Settings;
  • your advertising consent choices, stored on the device by the consent tool described in section 6;
  • whether the tutorial and the intro screens have already been seen.

None of this data identifies you as a person.

5. Servers and network traffic

We operate no servers of our own, and the game is fully playable offline — every level, your progress and your settings work without a connection. While Analytics is switched on, the App uploads the batched, encrypted analytics events listed in section 3 to Google's servers, together with the analytics SDK's own registration and configuration requests. The advertising SDK described in section 6 additionally contacts Google's ad servers to fetch and display an ad when one is offered, and to load your consent status. Without a connection, ads simply are not offered and the free path (restart, or play on without a hint) remains available.

6. Advertising

The App shows rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), together with Google's User Messaging Platform, which handles consent. Ads appear only in two places, and only when you ask for them: the "Watch ad" button on the Out of lives screen (which lets you continue the level with fresh lives), and the hint button once your free hint for a level is used. There are no banner ads, no ads between levels, and no ad that interrupts play.

When an ad is requested, Google receives from your device: your IP address (from which it derives an approximate location and which it uses to serve the ad), technical attributes of the device and the App (device model, operating-system version, language, app version and identifier), an identifier for this installation, information about the ads you are shown and whether you tap them, and non-identifying diagnostic information about the SDK's own operation. Google may also use its own information about you under the Google Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policies.

No tracking across apps. The App never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to Google through the App, and Google cannot use the App to link your activity here with your activity in other companies' apps or websites. On iOS this means the ads you see are not personalised on the basis of such cross-app data.

Your choices in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Before the first ad is shown, the App displays Google's consent dialog, where you can accept or decline personalised advertising and see the list of advertising partners. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Ad privacy in the game. If you decline, ads may still be shown but are limited to non-personalised ads. Elsewhere, the ad service operates under the applicable local rules; you can still decline every ad simply by not tapping the button that requests it.

Legal basis. Where consent is required by law (for example under the GDPR or the UK GDPR for personalised advertising), we rely on the consent you give in the dialog described above and you may withdraw it at any time. Otherwise we rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game through advertising that you explicitly opt into by tapping the ad button (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).

7. Third-party services inside the App

The App integrates two third-party SDKs that process data about your use of it, plus two platform features that are operated by the platform vendors rather than by us:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase. Google processes the events listed in section 3 on our behalf, so that we can read aggregated statistics in the Firebase and Google Analytics consoles. We have enabled no Google signals and no Google Ads link. Google's own handling of the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in Firebase's privacy documentation.
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform. Google serves the rewarded ads described in section 6 and manages your consent choices. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it receives when serving ads; see the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob's privacy information.
  • App store distribution. Apple may collect data about downloads, crashes, and device type as part of distributing the App. This processing is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy, not by this policy. We only ever see anonymous, aggregated statistics in the developer console.
  • Rating prompt. The App may ask the operating system to show its own built-in "rate this app" dialog. The dialog is rendered and handled by the platform; we receive no personal data from it, and your review is handled under the store's own terms.

8. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children, and it is not listed in the children's category of the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country): there is no registration, no chat and no free-text field, and nothing in the App would allow a child to disclose personal information to us or to third parties. The analytics described in section 3 records only gameplay events tied to a random installation identifier; no profile is built. Ads are limited to a general-audience ("G") content rating, are never shown unless the player taps the button that requests one, and are served without the advertising identifier. A parent who prefers to send nothing at all can switch Analytics off in Settings and simply not use the ad buttons.

9. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection.

Legal basis for analytics. Where the GDPR or the KVKK applies, we process the analytics data described in section 3 on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the game is played and in finding and fixing problems (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). You can object at any time, with immediate effect and without giving a reason, by switching Analytics off in Settings. The legal basis for advertising is described in section 6.

Exercising your rights. Write to ferhatozkan7@gmail.com. Please bear in mind that the analytics data is only ever tied to a random app-instance identifier: we hold no name, e-mail address or account that would tell us which records belong to you, and we cannot re-identify you from them. If you send us your app-instance identifier we will delete the matching records; otherwise the fastest and most complete route is to switch Analytics off and delete the App — the identifier is then gone, and the remaining events are deleted by Google at the end of the retention period described in section 10. For data that Google holds as an independent controller when serving ads, please use the controls in your Google account and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information. The delivery of ads by Google AdMob may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CCPA/CPRA only if personalised ads are enabled for you; you can opt out through the consent dialog described in section 6 where it is offered, and in all cases by not requesting ads.

You can erase all data the App has saved locally at any time by deleting the App from your device. Note that a device backup created by you (for example an iCloud backup) may still contain that local game data; such backups are controlled by you and by the platform vendor, not by us.

10. Data retention and security

We keep no database of our own. The analytics events are stored by Google on our behalf and are deleted automatically at the end of the retention period configured in our Google Analytics property, which is at most 14 months; aggregated reports that no longer relate to a single installation (for example "62% of players finish level 12") may be kept for longer. Data received by Google when serving ads is retained according to the Google Privacy Policy. Events are transmitted over encrypted (TLS) connections. Game data saved locally on your device is protected by the sandboxing and security mechanisms of your operating system.

11. International transfers

The analytics and advertising data is processed by Google on servers that may be located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Google relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the other safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy for such transfers. We operate no servers ourselves and see only the reports shown in the Google consoles.
