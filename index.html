Privacy Policy for SwingOut

Effective date: 19 September 2026 · Version 1.1

Developer: Leiso Studio · Contact: ferhatozkan7@gmail.com

EnglishTürkçe özet

1. Summary

SwingOut has no user accounts, no advertising and no tracking, and the game itself runs entirely on your device. The one exception is anonymous gameplay analytics: the App sends a small set of events — for example that level 12 was started, and that it was completed in 48 seconds with 14 moves — to Google Analytics for Firebase, so that we can see which levels are too hard and where players stop playing. Those events are tied to a randomly generated app-instance identifier, never to your name, e-mail address or an account, and they are never used to show you advertising. If you would rather send nothing at all, open Settings in the game and switch Analytics off; collection stops immediately.

2. Who we are

SwingOut ("the App", "the Game") is developed and published by Leiso Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information, including the analytics described in section 3. If you have any question about it, write to ferhatozkan7@gmail.com.

3. Information we collect

We ask you for nothing: the App has no registration, no login and no field in which you could type personal information. The App does not collect, request, or transmit:

  • names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses;
  • user accounts, logins, passwords, or social-network profiles;
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) — the App does not include Apple's AdSupport framework, so it cannot read the IDFA at all;
  • precise location — the App never uses Location Services and asks for no location permission;
  • contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any other device content;
  • crash logs, performance traces, or any other diagnostic data — the App contains no crash-reporting SDK;
  • payment or financial information — the App contains no purchases.

The App requests no runtime permissions and is not able to read personal content from your device.

What the analytics SDK does collect

To understand how the game is played, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase. While Analytics is switched on, the following leaves your device:

  • Installation identifiers. A randomly generated app-instance identifier created by the SDK, a Firebase installation identifier the SDK registers for this copy of the App, and, on iOS, the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV). They identify an installation, not a person; they disappear when you delete the App, and switching Analytics off in Settings discards them too.
  • The gameplay events we define. level_start, level_clear, level_fail, hint_used and theme_selected, with parameters such as the level number, elapsed seconds, number of moves, the minimum number of moves for that level, the star rating, the number of wrong moves, whether a hint was used, the rotation direction, whether the attempt was a retry, and the identifier of the selected visual theme.
  • Events the SDK measures automatically, such as first launch, app opens, app updates, sessions and session duration, and screen views.
  • Technical attributes of the installation: device model, operating system and version, app version, device language, and the store the App was installed from.
  • An approximate location — country, region and city — which Google derives from your device's masked IP address. The App never uses Apple's Location Services, and we never see your IP address.

No free text, no user identifier and no content you create is attached to these events. We do not set a user ID, we do not use Google signals, our Analytics property is not linked to any Google Ads account, and age, gender or interest categories are not available to us at all, because they would require the advertising identifier that the App does not collect.

You can turn this off. Open Settings in the game and switch Analytics off. Collection stops immediately, any events still waiting to be sent are discarded, and nothing further leaves your device. You can switch it back on at any time.

4. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves game data in the standard local storage of your operating system (Unity PlayerPrefs, which maps to UserDefaults on iOS and SharedPreferences on Android). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It consists only of:

  • level progress, star ratings, and best move/time results;
  • achievement progress;
  • the number of lives remaining in the level you are playing;
  • the state of a level you left half-finished, so you can resume it: the moves you made so far, the elapsed time and the blocked moves;
  • whether the App has already asked you to rate it;
  • preferences such as sound, music, haptics, and the selected visual theme;
  • whether you have switched Analytics off in Settings;
  • whether the tutorial and the intro screens have already been seen.

None of this data identifies you as a person.

5. Servers and network traffic

We operate no servers of our own, and the game is fully playable offline — every level, your progress and your settings work without a connection. While Analytics is switched on, the network traffic the App creates consists of the batched, encrypted upload of the analytics events listed in section 3 to Google's servers, together with the analytics SDK's own registration and configuration requests — it obtains the installation identifier and fetches its measurement settings. If you switch Analytics off in Settings, that upload stops and nothing leaves your device.

6. No advertising and no tracking

The current version of the App contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites. Concretely: the App does not link Apple's AdSupport framework, so it has no access to the advertising identifier (IDFA); Google signals are switched off; our Google Analytics property is not linked to any Google Ads account; ad personalisation is disabled; and no data is shared with advertising networks or data brokers. Because there is nothing to track, the App displays no App Tracking Transparency prompt. The App does contain one analytics SDK — Google Analytics for Firebase — and what it collects is described in sections 3 and 7.

7. Third-party services inside the App

The App integrates one third-party SDK that processes data about your use of it, plus two platform features that are operated by the platform vendors rather than by us:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase. Google processes the events listed in section 3 on our behalf, so that we can read aggregated statistics in the Firebase and Google Analytics consoles. We have enabled none of the product's advertising features: no Google signals, no Google Ads link, no ad personalisation, no advertising identifier. Google's own handling of the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in Firebase's privacy documentation.
  • App store distribution. Apple and Google may collect data about downloads, purchases, crashes, and device type as part of distributing the App. This processing is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy, not by this policy. We only ever see anonymous, aggregated statistics in the developer consoles.
  • Rating prompt. The App may ask the operating system to show its own built-in "rate this app" dialog. The dialog is rendered and handled by the platform; we receive no personal data from it, and your review is handled under the store's own terms.

8. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country): there is no registration, no chat, no free-text field and no advertising in the App, and nothing in it would allow a child to disclose personal information to us or to third parties. The analytics described in section 3 records only gameplay events tied to a random installation identifier; no advertising identifier is collected, no profile is built, and none of it is used for advertising or for age, gender or interest categories. A parent who prefers to send nothing at all can switch Analytics off in Settings.

9. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection.

Legal basis. Where the GDPR or the KVKK applies, we process the analytics data described in section 3 on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding how the game is played and in finding and fixing problems (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). You can object at any time, with immediate effect and without giving a reason, by switching Analytics off in Settings.

Exercising your rights. Write to ferhatozkan7@gmail.com. Please bear in mind that the analytics data is only ever tied to a random app-instance identifier: we hold no name, e-mail address or account that would tell us which records belong to you, and we cannot re-identify you from them. If you send us your app-instance identifier we will delete the matching records; otherwise the fastest and most complete route is to switch Analytics off and delete the App — the identifier is then gone, and the remaining events are deleted by Google at the end of the retention period described in section 10.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA.

You can erase all data the App has saved locally at any time by deleting the App from your device. Note that a device backup created by you (for example an iCloud or Google backup) may still contain that local game data; such backups are controlled by you and by the platform vendor, not by us.

10. Data retention and security

We keep no database of our own. The analytics events are stored by Google on our behalf and are deleted automatically at the end of the retention period configured in our Google Analytics property, which is at most 14 months; aggregated reports that no longer relate to a single installation (for example "62% of players finish level 12") may be kept for longer. Events are transmitted over encrypted (TLS) connections. Game data saved locally on your device is protected by the sandboxing and security mechanisms of your operating system.

11. International transfers

The analytics data is processed by Google on servers that may be located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Google relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the other safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy and in the Google Analytics for Firebase terms for such transfers. We operate no servers ourselves and see only the reports shown in the Google consoles.

12. Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that changes how information is handled — for example rewarded advertising, cloud saves, or leaderboards — we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that feature is released, and we will update the privacy declarations in the App Store and Google Play accordingly. Version 1.1 (19 September 2026) documents the gameplay analytics that ship in version 1.0 of the App. The current version of this document always lives at this address; earlier versions remain visible in the public change history of the repository that hosts this page.
