Privacy Policy for SwingOut

Effective date: 18 September 2026 · Version 1.0

Developer: Leiso Studio · Contact: ferhatozkan7@gmail.com

EnglishTürkçe özet

1. Summary

SwingOut does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

2. Who we are

SwingOut ("the App", "the Game") is developed and published by Leiso Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information. If you have any question about it, write to ferhatozkan7@gmail.com.

3. Information we collect

We collect no personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect, request, or transmit:

  • names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses;
  • user accounts, logins, passwords, or social-network profiles;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA / Google Advertising ID), or IP addresses;
  • location data;
  • contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any other device content;
  • usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or behavioural profiles;
  • payment or financial information.

The App requests no runtime permissions and is not able to read personal content from your device.

4. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves game data in the standard local storage of your operating system (Unity PlayerPrefs, which maps to UserDefaults on iOS and SharedPreferences on Android). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It consists only of:

  • level progress, star ratings, and best move/time results;
  • achievement progress;
  • the number of lives remaining in the level you are playing;
  • preferences such as sound, music, haptics, and the selected visual theme;
  • whether the tutorial and the intro screens have already been seen.

None of this data identifies you as a person.

5. No internet connection, no servers

The App works fully offline. It does not connect to any server operated by us, because we operate none. No data leaves your device through the App.

6. No advertising and no tracking

The current version of the App contains no advertising, no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking technologies of any kind. We do not track you across apps or websites, and the App does not display the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has nothing to track.

7. No third-party services inside the App

The current version of the App integrates no third-party SDKs that process personal data. Two platform features may nevertheless be involved, and they are operated by the platform vendors rather than by us:

  • App store distribution. Apple and Google may collect data about downloads, purchases, crashes, and device type as part of distributing the App. This processing is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy, not by this policy. We only ever see anonymous, aggregated statistics in the developer consoles.
  • Rating prompt. The App may ask the operating system to show its own built-in "rate this app" dialog. The dialog is rendered and handled by the platform; we receive no personal data from it, and your review is handled under the store's own terms.

8. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we also collect no personal data from children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and there is nothing in the App that would allow a child to disclose personal information to us or to third parties.

9. Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, transfer, sell, or delete. We do not sell or share personal information, and we never have.

You can erase all data the App has saved locally at any time by deleting the App from your device. Note that a device backup created by you (for example an iCloud or Google backup) may still contain that local game data; such backups are controlled by you and by the platform vendor, not by us.

10. Data retention and security

We retain no data, so there is no retention period and no database that could be breached. Local game data is protected by the sandboxing and security mechanisms of your operating system.

11. International transfers

No personal data is collected, therefore no personal data is transferred to any other country.

12. Changes to this policy

If we ever add a feature that changes how information is handled — for example rewarded advertising, analytics, cloud saves, or leaderboards — we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that feature is released, and we will update the privacy declarations in the App Store and Google Play accordingly. The current version of this document always lives at this address; earlier versions remain visible in the public change history of the repository that hosts this page.

13. Contact

Leiso Studio
E-mail: ferhatozkan7@gmail.com
We aim to answer privacy questions within 30 days.
